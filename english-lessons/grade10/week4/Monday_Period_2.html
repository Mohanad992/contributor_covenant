Sequence: two different questions

Chronological order

What order did the events happen in the story world?

earliest → latest

Narrative order

What order did the author choose to tell them?

page 1 → page 2 → page 3 …

Why writers rearrange time: to create suspense, reveal character gradually, control pacing, and connect present events with past causes.

Four narrative tools in this story

In medias res

The story opens after the disaster has already happened, with Azucena trapped.

Backfill

The narrator later explains the eruption and destruction that created the situation.

Shift in focus

The narration moves between Azucena, Rolf, the media, and the narrator watching from afar.

Flashback

Rolf’s present experience opens into memories from childhood.

Instruction & Modeling
Grade 10 • Unit 1: At Home and Away
The story opens with Azucena already trapped

Sequence move 1: start inside the crisis

Effect: the reader meets the human consequence first. We see a trapped child before receiving the full explanation of the disaster.
Why this works: the opening creates immediate emotional urgency, then the background information answers the reader’s question: “How did this happen?”
Instruction & Modeling
Grade 10 • Unit 1: At Home and Away
The narration moves backward to explain the disaster

Sequence move 2: backfill the cause

Warnings → eruption → mudslide → buried villages → rescue response
Function: this section provides the chronological cause of the crisis after the reader has already seen its consequence.
Turn and talk: Would the opening feel different if the story began with the geologists instead of Azucena? Why?
Instruction & Modeling
Grade 10 • Unit 1: At Home and Away
Rolf is introduced through his history as a reporter

Sequence move 3: shift from disaster to Rolf

The narration pauses the immediate rescue to explain Rolf’s professional history and emotional distance.

Purpose: this information prepares us to notice the later change. Without the “before” portrait, his emotional collapse would have less meaning.
Instruction & Modeling
Grade 10 • Unit 1: At Home and Away
The narrator watches television while Rolf is at the disaster site

Sequence move 4: two places at once

The story alternates between Rolf at the scene and the narrator following events through television.

Effect: the structure keeps the public media event and the private emotional experience side by side. The distance of the screen contrasts with Rolf’s physical closeness to Azucena.
Instruction & Modeling
Grade 10 • Unit 1: At Home and Away
Rolf’s memories interrupt the present-time rescue

Sequence move 5: flashback

“memory poured out”

The story leaves the present rescue and enters memories of war, family, fear, and his sister.

Purpose: chronology is interrupted so the reader can understand why Azucena affects Rolf so deeply.

Wrap-Up / Reflection
Grade 10 • Unit 1: At Home and Away
The ending returns to the narrator and the changed Rolf

Why the structure matters

The story begins with a public disaster but gradually reveals a private one inside Rolf.

Sequence creates meaning: the rescue is not only a chain of physical events. By delaying the childhood history until Rolf is emotionally exposed, Allende makes the past feel as though it is breaking into the present.
