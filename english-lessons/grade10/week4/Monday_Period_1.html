Warm-Up⏱ 5 min
Conflict is not simply “a problem.” Ask: Where is the struggle happening?
🌍

External conflict

A character struggles against a force outside the self: another person, nature, society, or circumstances.

🧠

Internal conflict

A character struggles within the self: fear, guilt, memory, values, desires, or a difficult decision.

Quick pair task: Give one example of each from a film, story, or real-life situation.
Learning Objectives⏱ 2 min

By the end of the lesson, I can…

01

Classify

Distinguish external conflict from internal conflict accurately.

02

Trace

Explain how an external event can trigger an internal struggle.

03

Prove

Use textual evidence to justify the classification and relationship.

Success criterion: Name the conflict + identify the opposing forces + cite evidence + explain the link.
Instruction & Modeling — I Do⏱ 8 min

Model 1: Locate the struggle

Event: Azucena is trapped and rescue attempts fail.

Opposing forces: Rolf / rescuers vs. mud, debris, limited equipment, and time.

Classification: External conflict because the obstacle exists outside Rolf.

Ask: “Could this struggle still exist if Rolf felt completely calm?” If yes, the obstacle itself is external.

Textbook page: failed rescue attempts and the need for a pump
Instruction & Modeling — I Do⏱ 7 min

Model 2: Trace the external → internal chain

External pressure: he cannot free Azucena.

↓

Emotional trigger: his confidence and professional distance weaken.

↓

Internal conflict: he confronts helplessness, painful memories, and vulnerability.

A strong analysis does not merely name two conflicts. It explains how the external situation causes or intensifies the internal struggle.
Textbook page: the rescue environment and Rolf’s deepening personal involvement

Guided Practice — We Do⏱ 6 min

Which is the strongest internal-conflict evidence?

Evidence Formula

Conflict: Rolf experiences internal conflict as he confronts helplessness and past trauma.
Evidence: his emotional defenses weaken and buried memories surface.
Reasoning: this shows the struggle is occurring inside him, even though the rescue crisis triggers it.
Independent Practice — You Do⏱ 8 min

Wrap-Up / Reflection⏱ 4 min
Finish the chain:

External obstacle → ______ → Internal struggle

Fill the middle with the trigger or emotional response that connects the two.

Exit check: If you can explain the arrow between the two conflicts, you have moved from identification to analysis.
