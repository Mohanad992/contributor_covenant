Instruction & Modeling — I Do⏱ 8 min

Skill 1: Context changes how we interpret the opening

  • Locate: where and when is the disaster?
  • Measure: which details show scale and danger?
  • Connect: how does that context shape the opening image of Azucena?
Worked model: The background identifies a volcanic mudslide that devastated Armero. When the story then opens with Azucena trapped in clay, the reader understands immediately that her situation is part of a large-scale catastrophe rather than an isolated accident.
Context prepares us to understand the urgency of the rescue, the media presence, and the limits of what Rolf can do.

Instruction & Modeling — I Do⏱ 7 min

Skill 2: Infer vocabulary with a 3-step context test

  1. Read the sentence before and after the word.
  2. Propose a simple synonym.
  3. Put the synonym back into the sentence. Does the meaning still fit?
“...assess the magnitude of the cataclysm...”

Model thinking: nearby details describe towns buried by mud, massive destruction, and many deaths. Therefore, cataclysm means a large-scale disaster.
